What could be more invigorating than moments of crisp mountain air interspersed with moments of wonder, intrigue and discovery?

Twenty members, most of them a short walk apart. Select a name or a number on the map to read its label, or watch the film and experience us vicariously.

  4. Established in 1974, Canada House Gallery is dedicated to showcasing the best of contemporary Canadian art alongside a comprehensive collection of Inuit art from Canada's Arctic.

    WebsiteFind us

  5. The Indigenous peoples of Canada welcome you to share their history, culture and knowledge in this heritage log building in Banff. By connecting with each other on sacred land, you are inspiring the practice of truth and reconciliation. Named a Top 10 Indigenous experience in Alberta by Indigenous Travel Alberta. Are you curious?

    WebsiteFind us

  6. In the heart of Banff, Bison Courtyard brings together locally owned shops, cafés and galleries in a sunlit outdoor setting. Designed with care for the environment and community, it is a place to gather, explore and discover local creativity year-round.

    WebsiteFind us

  7. Exclusively features the work of Indigenous visual artist Jason Carter. His bright paintings and bold soapstone sculpture are permanent installations at both the Calgary and Edmonton International Airports.

  8. A volunteer committee that animates the Banff townsite through its work. Take a walk around and experience Banff's varied public art collection.

  9. Visit the birthplace of national parks in Canada. Discover the hot water that seeps from the rocks, smell the minerals and explore the trails. Immerse yourself in exhibits and programs that bring history and nature to life.

    WebsiteFind us

  10. Full of secrets and stories from early Banff. The Luxton family were adventurers, entrepreneurs and collectors. Discover Alberta artwork, historic beadwork, vintage furniture and more than a few taxidermy specimens. Each room is bursting with keepsakes, trinkets and surprises.

    WebsiteFind us

  11. Bowstrings Heritage Foundation weaves together past and present, story and place, to inspire the future. Working in partnership with others to advance public appreciation for heritage, and to restore and animate heritage sites.

    Website

  12. Discover the art, history and culture of the Rocky Mountains of Canada. The Whyte acquires, preserves and interprets the rich heritage of the Rockies through its collections, exhibitions and programming.

    WebsiteFind us

  13. One of Canada's leading contemporary art galleries, located within the Fairmont hotels in Whistler, Banff and Jasper. Since 1992, a curated selection of paintings, sculpture, photography and ceramics from established and emerging Canadian artists, reflecting the landscapes and stories of the Canadian wilderness.

    WebsiteFind us

  14. A cornerstone of the community that stimulates lifelong learning, fosters connections, promotes discovery and encourages a sense of belonging. A welcoming meeting place as well as a source of education, communication and information.

    WebsiteFind us

  15. Working towards the protection, preservation, enhancement and promotion of Banff's heritage resources. A broad membership whose activities support the Banff Heritage Resource Management Policy and the identification and management of local cultural assets.

  16. In addition to appointing the local Poet Laureate, the committee promotes and advocates for written words, read, spoken and sung. Bringing together Banff residents, visitors and neighbours for readings and performances, literary walking tours and workshops.

  17. Photographing mostly in the Canadian Rockies, Brandon captures images of wild, free-roaming animals without the use of any baiting. A "wildlife first" approach results in more natural behaviour and positive encounters. His award-winning images have been featured by Canadian Geographic and others.

Banff Culture Collection

A volunteer partnership of Banff's galleries, museums, heritage sites, festivals, artists and animators, active for more than 30 years. Formerly the Banff Culture Collective.

We live, work and play in the traditional territory of the Treaty 7 Nations.
