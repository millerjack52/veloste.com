About the Collection

A volunteer group, active for more than 30 years, working in partnership to raise Banff's profile as an arts, cultural, heritage and natural history destination.

Our vision is a vibrant, diverse, resilient cultural community that reflects Banff's unique place in the world.

How the Collection works

Every member is an independent gallery, museum, heritage site, festival, committee, artist or shop. Together they share one goal: to help residents and visitors discover the depth of mountain arts, culture and heritage in Banff.

The Collection is not a ticketed attraction. It is a map of what is here, kept by the people who run these places. Each entry in the catalogue links straight to the member.

A meeting place for all peoples.

We live, work and play in the traditional territory of the Treaty 7 Nations, in a place where Indigenous peoples met for centuries to exchange and trade.

Today, Banff is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Canada's flagship national park, and a great place to discover mountain arts and culture. Several members of the Collection, including Buffalo Nations Museum and the Indigenous art collections in town, share this history directly with visitors.
