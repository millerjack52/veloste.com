Privacy + Terms of Use

This policy explains how and why we collect and use site visitor information, consistent with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act.

Your information

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand the interests of site visitors and to help you navigate. You can set your browser to notify you when you receive a cookie, giving you the chance to decide whether to accept it. You can also set your browser to turn off cookies.

Our website files and the servers we use are protected by passwords and other security measures. They are accessible only by people who require the information to do work that we have asked them to do.

Social media

We may use social media as another tool to share information and build relationships. Social media accounts are public and are not hosted on our servers. They have their own terms of service and privacy policies, which you should review if you have questions about how your personal information is used via these channels.

Changes to this policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will post them here. Any changes will be effective as of the date we post them.

Photography and artwork

Photographs and artwork on this site are used with the permission of Paul Zizka and the member organisations of the Banff Culture Collection, and remain the property of their respective owners.

Banff Culture Collection

A volunteer partnership of Banff's galleries, museums, heritage sites, festivals, artists and animators, active for more than 30 years. Formerly the Banff Culture Collective.

We live, work and play in the traditional territory of the Treaty 7 Nations.
