Technology, ownership, and handoff

Make the technical decisions serve the website.

01

The stack follows the job

Veloste works with modern front-end tools including React and TypeScript, and can build real-time 3D experiences when the idea earns the added complexity. The platform decision starts with content, editing needs, integrations, performance, and the team that will maintain the site - not with a favourite tool forced onto every project.

02

Ownership is defined before launch

The proposal identifies the deliverables and responsibilities for hosting, domains, analytics, content, third-party accounts, and post-launch work. That gives your team a practical handoff instead of discovering after launch that a critical account or integration was never assigned.

03

Accessibility and measurement matter

Semantic structure, keyboard use, responsive behaviour, readable hierarchy, and useful alternatives are considered during design and development. When analytics access and conversion definitions are part of scope, the launch can also measure meaningful actions such as qualified form submissions, calls, and email enquiries.

Who leads the work

Direct access to a small, in-house team.

Veloste is led by founder and director Jack Miller. Strategy, design, and development stay closely connected so important decisions do not disappear between sales, account management, design, and an outsourced production team.

The model is intentionally focused: understand the business, agree on the scope, and build the system the project requires. If a need falls outside that scope, it is identified before implementation rather than hidden behind a generic package.

Frequently asked questions

Before you hire a Calgary web developer.

What does a custom website cost in Calgary?

Veloste quotes each project after discovery because cost depends on page count, content readiness, integrations, animation, and technical requirements. The proposal sets out the deliverables, phases, and price before work begins.

How long does a web development project take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, review cycles, and integrations. Veloste provides a delivery schedule in the proposal and confirms milestones before the build starts.

Does Veloste use website templates?

No. Veloste designs and develops each website around the client's goals, audience, brand, and conversion path rather than re-skinning a prebuilt theme.

Is technical SEO included?

Technical foundations such as semantic structure, metadata, crawl paths, responsive behaviour, and performance are considered during the build. Any ongoing content or authority campaign is scoped separately.

Can we meet in Calgary?

Yes. Veloste is Calgary-based and can arrange local collaboration when it is useful for the project. Remote delivery is also available across Canada and the United States.

What happens after launch?

Veloste supports launch-related fixes after completion. Ongoing optimization, new features, design updates, and maintenance can be arranged as a separate support scope.
