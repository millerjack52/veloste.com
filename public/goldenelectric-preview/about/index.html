About Us

Meet the Golden Electrical team

Local electricians bringing broad experience, dependable workmanship, and a practical approach to every project.

Nick Krywenko

Founder · Red Seal Electrician · Class A Master FSR

Nick started his electrical career in Australia more than 20 years ago. He worked his way up to project manager on large-scale commercial construction, including data centres, commercial buildings, hospitals, and defence sites. He also worked as a climbing guide before finding the work-life balance he was looking for in Golden.

Nick launched Golden Electrical Contracting Ltd. in 2018 to provide a reliable local contractor with a diverse range of services. Away from work, he is usually exploring the mountains and backcountry with his fiancée and their two dogs.
