Ask Us a Question

Electrical permits are required for many installations and alterations in British Columbia. They create a record of the work, support safety oversight, and help confirm that regulated work is completed by an authorized person. When you hire us for permitted work, we take care of the permit application.

Electrical permit information from Technical Safety BC

Available capacity depends on your existing service size and current electrical loads. We can complete the required load calculation and explain whether the project fits your present service or requires an upgrade. We offer this initial assessment free of charge.

Solar can reduce the electricity you purchase from the grid over time. The right system depends on roof exposure, current energy use, equipment cost, available incentives, and your long-term goals. Typical payback can range from 10 to 15 years, while many solar panels carry 25-year performance warranties.

Battery systems and manual or automatic standby generators can keep essential loads running when grid power is unavailable. We can help size the system and choose an option based on the equipment and circuits you need to support.

Service size depends on heating, hot water, appliances, EV charging, hot tubs, secondary suites, and future plans. We complete a load calculation and coordinate the appropriate service size before installation. We offer this calculation free of charge.

Options for an electrical upgrade depend on your home's current power capacity, physical breaker space, and future energy needs like adding a heat pump, electric vehicle charger, or even a hot tub. Please contact us for a free in-home assessment and calculation based on your needs.

Cost varies with the existing equipment, required service size, utility requirements, trenching or overhead work, panel location, and site access. Contact us for a free project-specific quote.
