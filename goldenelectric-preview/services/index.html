Services

Services we offer

You name it, we do it. From design-build homes and renovations to multi-unit commercial projects, restaurants, solar, turbine, and off-grid systems, we handle electrical work across every scale.

Our licensed and insured electricians bring broad experience, practical problem-solving, and current safety knowledge to every repair, upgrade, and complex installation.

Service Installations

Complete electrical service installations that safely connect properties to BC Hydro’s power grid, including exterior connections, meters, main distribution panels, and grounding systems.

Service Upgrades

Increase the capacity of your electrical service to safely accommodate larger loads, improve reliability, and meet the changing demands of your home or business.

New Home Builds

Complete electrical planning and installation for new homes, thoughtfully integrated with the building design and completed to current codes and safety standards.

Renovations

Practical electrical solutions for renovations of every size, including assessments, rewiring, panel work, new circuits, lighting, and upgrades for modern equipment.

Solar Installations

Solar systems that convert sunlight into usable electricity, designed around the property, energy needs, and long-term goals of each project. We have extensive installation experience throughout the Columbia Valley.

Alternative Energy Solutions

Renewable and non-conventional energy systems that reduce dependence on the grid and support more resilient, efficient, and sustainable power generation.

Backup Power Options

Battery storage and manual or automatic generators that keep essential equipment running when the primary power supply is unavailable or disrupted.

Hot Tubs

Safe electrical infrastructure for hot tubs and spas, including dedicated circuits, disconnects, grounding, bonding, and equipment connections.

EV Charging Stations

Electric vehicle charging equipment for homes and businesses, with the load calculations, service assessment, wiring, and protection required for a safe installation.

Commercial Buildings

Electrical systems for offices, retail spaces, restaurants, hotels, and other commercial facilities. Our team has designed and built projects throughout Golden and the surrounding area.

Industrial Buildings

Electrical infrastructure for industrial processes and equipment, including automation, motors, processing equipment, programmable logic controllers, and variable-frequency drives.

High Voltage

Golden Electrical Contracting is Class A certified to work on all voltages, including high-voltage systems above 750 volts.
