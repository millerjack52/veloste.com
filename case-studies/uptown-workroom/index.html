Case study / Brand website

Uptown Workroom

A bold website for a Dunedin screen printing and embroidery workroom, connecting brand energy with a practical route from service discovery to customisation and quote.

  • ClientUptown Workroom
  • LocationDunedin, New Zealand
  • IndustryScreen printing and embroidery
  • ScopeStrategy, UX, art direction, development

The challenge

Make a hands-on workroom feel immediate online.

Uptown Workroom brings brands onto apparel, uniforms, promotional products, and transfers. The website needed to present those capabilities clearly without losing the physical, energetic character of the work.

It also needed to support two kinds of visitors: people comparing production services and teams already thinking about garments, quantities, finishes, and a quote. The structure had to make both paths easy to follow.

The approach

A visual system built from the work itself.

Veloste combined oversized typography, workshop imagery, direct service language, and compact navigation to create a site that feels confident without getting in the way of the information.

Service sections explain screen printing, embroidery, promotional products, and transfers. A dedicated online customisation pathway gives teams a clear next step, while the quote flow brings the conversation back to practical project details.

What Veloste delivered

A focused website system, not a disconnected set of pages.

  • Service architectureClear paths for four production capabilities
  • Art directionBold type, motion, image treatment, and colour
  • Responsive interfaceDesigned for desktop and mobile use
  • Customisation pathwayA clearer route from product idea to quote
  • Local search structureSemantic service and location signals
  • Front-end developmentCustom interaction and presentation code

The outcome

A more distinctive and usable expression of the workroom.

The result is a website concept that gives Uptown Workroom's services a clearer hierarchy, shows the physical craft through real imagery, and connects browsing to a concrete customisation and quote path.
