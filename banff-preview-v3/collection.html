    Banff Centre Mountain Film and Book Festival

    Film & Words · Festival · Banff Centre

    An international competition and annual presentation of short films, books and documentaries about mountain culture, sports and the environment. Hosted at the Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity every fall, and available to experience through its Virtual World Tour.

    Walter Phillips Gallery

    Galleries · Gallery · Banff Centre

    Located within the Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity. Since 1976, committed to the production, presentation, collection and analysis of contemporary art and curatorial practice.

    Willock & Sax Gallery

    Galleries · Gallery · 210 Bear St

    Fine art you will love for the rest of your life. This well-curated gallery has a selection of work from painters, photographers, printmakers, ceramic artists, sculptors and jewellers.

  4. 04

    Canada House Gallery

    Galleries · Gallery · 201 Bear St

    Established in 1974, Canada House Gallery is dedicated to showcasing the best of contemporary Canadian art alongside a comprehensive collection of Inuit art from Canada's Arctic.

    ExploreFind us

  5. 05

    Buffalo Nations Museum

    Museums · Museum · 1 Birch Ave

    The Indigenous peoples of Canada welcome you to share their history, culture and knowledge in this heritage log building in Banff. By connecting with each other on sacred land, you are inspiring the practice of truth and reconciliation. Named a Top 10 Indigenous experience in Alberta by Indigenous Travel Alberta. Are you curious?

    ExploreFind us

    Bison Courtyard

    Community · Courtyard · 211 Bear St

    In the heart of Banff, Bison Courtyard brings together locally owned shops, cafés and galleries in a sunlit outdoor setting. Designed with care for the environment and community, it is a place to gather, explore and discover local creativity year-round.

    ExploreFind us

  7. 07

    The Carter-Ryan Gallery

    Galleries · Gallery · 229 Bear St

    Exclusively features the work of Indigenous visual artist Jason Carter. His bright paintings and bold soapstone sculpture are permanent installations at both the Calgary and Edmonton International Airports.

    ExploreFind us

  8. 08

    Town of Banff Community Art Committee

    Community · Public art · Townsite

    A volunteer committee that animates the Banff townsite through its work. Take a walk around and experience Banff's varied public art collection.

  9. 09

    Cave and Basin National Historic Site

    Museums · Historic site · 311 Cave Ave

    Visit the birthplace of national parks in Canada. Discover the hot water that seeps from the rocks, smell the minerals and explore the trails. Immerse yourself in exhibits and programs that bring history and nature to life.

    ExploreFind us

  10. 10

    Historic Luxton Home Museum

    Museums · Museum · Beaver St

    Full of secrets and stories from early Banff. The Luxton family were adventurers, entrepreneurs and collectors. Discover Alberta artwork, historic beadwork, vintage furniture and more than a few taxidermy specimens. Each room is bursting with keepsakes, trinkets and surprises.

    ExploreFind us

    Bowstrings Banff

    Museums · Heritage foundation · Banff

    Bowstrings Heritage Foundation weaves together past and present, story and place, to inspire the future. Working in partnership with others to advance public appreciation for heritage, and to restore and animate heritage sites.

    Explore

    Whyte Museum of the Canadian Rockies

    Museums · Museum · 111 Bear St

    Discover the art, history and culture of the Rocky Mountains of Canada. The Whyte acquires, preserves and interprets the rich heritage of the Rockies through its collections, exhibitions and programming.

    ExploreFind us

  13. 13

    Mountain Galleries

    Galleries · Gallery · Fairmont Banff Springs

    One of Canada's leading contemporary art galleries, located within the Fairmont hotels in Whistler, Banff and Jasper. Since 1992, a curated selection of paintings, sculpture, photography and ceramics from established and emerging Canadian artists, reflecting the landscapes and stories of the Canadian wilderness.

    Banff Public Library

    Community · Library · 101 Bear St

    Town of Banff Heritage Corporation

    Museums · Heritage · Banff

    Working towards the protection, preservation, enhancement and promotion of Banff's heritage resources. A broad membership whose activities support the Banff Heritage Resource Management Policy and the identification and management of local cultural assets.

  16. 16

    Banff Poet Laureate Committee

    Film & Words · Words · Banff

    In addition to appointing the local Poet Laureate, the committee promotes and advocates for written words, read, spoken and sung. Bringing together Banff residents, visitors and neighbours for readings and performances, literary walking tours and workshops.

    Explore

  17. 17

    Brandon T. Brown

    Galleries · Photographer · Canadian Rockies

    Photographing mostly in the Canadian Rockies, Brandon captures images of wild, free-roaming animals without the use of any baiting. A "wildlife first" approach results in more natural behaviour and positive encounters. His award-winning images have been featured by Canadian Geographic and others.

    ExploreFind us

    Banff Park Museum National Historic Site

    Museums · Historic site · 91 Banff Ave

    Explore the oldest natural history museum in Western Canada and get face-to-face with Rocky Mountain wildlife. In the heart of downtown Banff, the museum houses more than 5,000 vintage botanical and zoological specimens, from bears and bighorns to birds and bees.
